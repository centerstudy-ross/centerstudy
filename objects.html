Study Objects
The work does not end when the session does.
These are objects made to keep practice close: things to hold, write in, eat from, and return to. Each one extends the centerstudy approach into daily life, where the body does most of its learning.

Workbooks & Printed Matter
Printed workbooks drawn from centerstudy workshops, with prompts, body maps, and exercises for writing from center. Made to be written in, dog-eared, and carried. Alongside them: zines and small-run publications.
Ritual Ware
Handmade ceramic vessels, bowls, dishes, and candle holders, made as ritual ware. Each piece is an extension of my facilitation practice: objects for tea, for offering, for feeding and being fed, for marking a beginning or an ending. Surfaces are hand-painted with underglaze. Pieces are made in small batches and vary from one to the next.
Digital
Downloadable worksheets, templates, and guides for artists writing statements, proposals, and applications, including tools for finding and tracking grants, residencies, and open calls.
